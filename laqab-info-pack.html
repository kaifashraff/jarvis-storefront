✨ Our Story

LAQAB — an Urdu word meaning title, honour, or epithet — was born from a simple belief: every bride deserves to feel like a queen.

What started as a small atelier in the heart of Old Delhi's embroidery district has grown into a trusted name for handcrafted bridal wear. Our master artisans — many with over 25 years of experience — bring generations of zardozi, resham, and gotta patti expertise to every piece we create.

We don't just stitch fabric. We weave stories, preserve traditions, and create heirlooms that brides pass down to their daughters.

From the first sketch to the final fitting, every LAQAB piece is a promise: craftsmanship that honours the past, designed for the bride of today.

"Your wedding day is the overture to your forever. Wear something that deserves the applause."

❓ FAQs — for NRI Customers

Do you ship internationally? What are the shipping costs?
Yes! We ship worldwide. Shipping costs vary by destination and package weight. Typically: USA/Canada — ₹2,500-3,500 ($30-42), UK/Europe — ₹2,000-3,000 ($24-36), UAE/GCC — ₹1,500-2,000 ($18-24), Australia — ₹2,500-3,500 ($30-42). We use reliable courier services (DHL/FedEx/DTDC) with full tracking.
How do I get measured if I'm not in India?
We provide a detailed measurement guide with photos. You can get measured at any local tailor, or we can walk you through it via a WhatsApp video call. For best results, send us a well-fitted garment of similar style and we take measurements from it. We also offer fabric swatch courier to your international address (nominal fee).
What payment methods do you accept from NRIs?
We accept international bank transfers (SWIFT/IBAN), Wise (TransferWise), PayPal (+4% fee), Google Pay (for Indian accounts), and major credit/debit cards through our payment gateway. All transactions are secure and we provide proper invoices.
What if the outfit doesn't fit? Return/exchange policy?
Since every piece is made-to-order, we cannot accept general returns. However, if there's a defect or sizing error on our part, we will fix it free of charge (including shipping). For minor fit issues, we can guide your local tailor through adjustments via video. We recommend ordering 6-8 weeks before your event to allow time for any adjustments.
Are there customs/duties I should expect?
Import duties and taxes are borne by the customer and vary by country. We declare the actual value on customs forms and provide all necessary documentation. USA has a duty-free threshold of $800 for personal shipments. UK/EU customers should expect 20-30% duties+VAT in most cases. We advise checking with your local customs office.
How long in advance should I order for a destination wedding?
We recommend ordering 8-12 weeks before your event for international orders. This gives us 3-4 weeks for production, 1 week for fittings, 1-2 weeks for shipping, and buffer time for any adjustments. Rush orders (3-4 weeks) are possible with a 15% surcharge.
Can I see fabric/embroidery samples before ordering?
Absolutely! We can courier a fabric swatch card and embroidery sample to your international address for ₹1,000 ($12) — refundable upon order. You can also join a WhatsApp video call where we show you available fabrics and current work in progress.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations are accepted within 48 hours of order placement with a full refund (minus payment gateway fees). After 48 hours, if production has started (fabric cutting/embroidery begun), the advance payment is non-refundable. We always share production updates so you know the status.
