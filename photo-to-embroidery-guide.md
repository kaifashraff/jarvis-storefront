## Why Artwork Matters
Bad source art = messy stitches. Clean art = crisp embroidery.

## Step 1 — Get the Right File
- Vector (SVG, AI, EPS) is best — scales without blur.
- Raster only if 300 DPI at final size (PNG/JPG).
- Avoid screenshots & low-res WhatsApp images.

## Step 2 — Simplify
- Merge tiny details (< 3mm won't stitch cleanly).
- Limit colours to what threads can match.
- Remove gradients (embroidery uses solid thread colours).

## Step 3 — Brief Your Digitizer
- Final size (cm), item type (cap/chest/lehenga).
- Preferred thread (rayon/polyester), placement.
- Usage (one-off vs bulk).

## File Formats We Accept
SVG, PNG (300dpi), JPG, PDF, AI, EPS.
